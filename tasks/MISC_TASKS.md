| M39 | Give Classic its own main-window visualizer mode key | Classic and Original share `mainWindowVisMode`. `ModernMainWindowView` writes it alongside its own `modernMainWindowVisMode` on every mode change, and the key doubles as the command channel: the context menu (`MenuActions.setMainVisMode`) writes only it, both main-window views read it in `mainVisSettingsChanged`, and `ModernSkinEngine` and `VisualizationPreferences` write both keys. Classic can't tell its own choice from Original's, so entering Classic (`prepareUIRuntime`) overwrites it with Spectrum, and a mode picked in Classic is lost after a trip through Original. Split the key so each family persists its own mode, and route the menu's change through whichever family is running. **Done when** Classic's main-window mode survives Classic → Original → Classic, Original's survives the reverse, the menu checkmark is right in both, and the Spectrum overwrite on entering Classic is deleted. Skills: `main-window-visualization`, `app-state`, `original-skin-guide`. |
| M40 | Plex Artists tab waits for every album before showing any artist | Measured 2026-10-13 against NAS3-PLEX (988 artists, 6366 albums), Original and Classic alike: the first Artists tab after launch stays on the spinner for 6–7 s. `LibraryBrowserModel+Plex` `loadPlexDataForCurrentMode` `.artists` assigns `plex.artists` and `plex.albums` together, either from `PlexManager`'s preload or from `fetchArtists()` then `fetchAllAlbums()`. The artists arrive in about 1 s and the albums take the other 5–6 s, so nothing shows until both are in (the rows appeared 0.3 s after `Stored preloaded music`). `main` does the same, so this is not a regression. The albums only feed the per-artist album counts and the same-name grouping (`buildArtistAlbumCounts`), which is why the two are kept atomic. **Done when** the Artists tab lists artists once they have loaded, the album-count column and same-name groups fill in when the albums land without re-ordering or duplicating rows, and a superseded load still cannot leave artists without their albums. Skills: `plex-integration`, `local-library` (browser model). |

What draws it is not known yet: the border itself is `SkinRenderer.drawPlexBrowserStatusBar`, deterministic for a given bounds. **Done when** the drawing site is identified and every Plex tab's bottom border matches the Local one across two launches. Skills: `ui-guide`, `live-ui-testing`. |
| M43 | Local shows and seasons take art only from their first episode | Read from code 2026-10-13, not yet reproduced live. `LibraryBrowserArt.itemArtwork` `.localShow` uses the first episode of the first non-empty season and `.localSeason` its first episode, through `LocalArtwork.image(for:)`; Plex, Jellyfin and Emby shows and seasons carry their own art. A show whose first episode has no embedded art falls back only to that episode's folder image (`LocalArtwork` knows `cover`/`folder`/`front`/`album`, not `poster`), even when a later episode, or a `poster.jpg` beside the files, has art. Extend `LocalArtwork` with a show resolver: embedded art from any episode, then a folder poster (`poster.jpg`, `folder.jpg`), then `LibraryArtwork.moviePoster` by show title. **Done when** a local show and season whose first episode has no art show a poster in the library browser rows, Flow / Tiles and the background. Skills: `local-library`, `album-art-visualizer`. |
| M44 | Casting a local track sends no cover | Read from code 2026-10-13. `CastManager.castArtworkURL` is `track.serverArtworkURL(size:)`, nil for a local file, so Sonos, Chromecast and DLNA receivers show no art for local tracks even when `LocalArtwork.image(for:)` finds one. The receiver needs an http URL: serve the resolved image from `LocalMediaServer`, which already serves local audio to receivers. **Done when** a local track with embedded art, and one with only a folder image, show their cover on a Sonos and a Chromecast receiver. Skills: `sonos-casting`, `chromecast-casting`, `local-library`. |
